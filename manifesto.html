"In the stillness of winter, we choose warmth.
In the dark, we kindle light.
In solitude, we make and rest.
In company, we feast and laugh.
This is Cozyfest."

Purpose: To celebrate comfort, warmth, and light in the heart of winter

Duration: 5 weekends, starting the second weekend after New Year's (Jan 8 – Feb 7, 2027)

The Aesthetic & Mood

"Deep midnight blues, quiet amber light, steamy mugs, and warm wood. A sanctuary built against the winter cold."

  • Atmosphere: Low-lit rooms illuminated by candlelight, lantern glow, and starlight.
  • Textures: Heavy wool blankets, worn paper, crackling fires, and simmering spices.
  • Pacing: Intentional slowness—choosing warmth, quiet contemplation, and gentle connection.

The Five-Weekend Framework

1. Kickoff: "The Kindling"

1/8 – 1/10/2027

  • Buy one new cozy item: sweater, blanket, socks, slippers.
  • Choose your signature cozy drink: tea, cocoa, wine.
  • Have comforting soup or stew for dinner.
  • Light a candle and let yourself feel peace.

2. Read-a-thon Weekend

1/15 – 1/18/2027

  • Buy or borrow an exciting book.
  • Target 12–24 hours of reading over the MLK weekend.
  • Cozy setting required: blanket, warm beverage, low light.

3. Feast Weekend

1/22 – 1/24/2027

  • Invite loved ones for a hearty braised meat, stew, or soup.
  • Candlelight, long table, slow pace.
  • Everyone brings something warming: bread, wine, dessert, or a story.

4. Making Weekend

1/29 – 1/31/2027

  • Engage hands to calm mind: Legos, puzzles, crochet, models, baking.
  • Bonus points for ambient playlists and warm lighting.
  • Host a "Craft & Cocoa" night with friends.

5. Movie Weekend

2/5 – 2/7/2027

  • Movie marathon at home or a cozy outing to a theater.
  • Snacks are essential — ideally homemade popcorn or baked treats.
