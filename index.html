In the stillness of winter, we choose warmth.

Cozyfest is a 5-weekend holiday dedicated to permission: to rest, to make slowly, to share warmth, and to find light in small rituals when the world exhales after the holidays.

Seasonal Challenges

Complete these before the festival ends to fully embrace the winter chill:

  • ☐ Bake something, sweet or savory — warmth through creation
  • ☐ Build a pillow fort — for reading, napping, or movie watching
  • ☐ Make homemade ice cream — a playful nod to winter chill
  • ☐ Practice self-care — massage, face mask, fancy lotion, or cozy bath
